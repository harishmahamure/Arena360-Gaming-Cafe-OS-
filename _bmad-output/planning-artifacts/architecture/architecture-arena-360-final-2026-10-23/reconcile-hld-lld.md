# Reconcile: HLD/LLD → Spine (Finalize step 2)

HLD = `docs/Arena360 — High-Level Design.md` · LLD = `docs/Arena360 — Low-Level Design.md`.
These are load-bearing items in the HLD/LLD that the spine does not carry. They exclude items the memlog marks as superseded: dynamic UPI QR and branch keys, the Kotlin shell, the Flutter owner app, phone+PIN login, `wallet.reward` and rewarded ads, per-branch art, the `LEASE_FRESH` rejection, kiosk time from `session.tick` only, Win7/C#/WPF, and the 4 s Pi switch.

Each item is tagged **AD** (add a new AD or amend one) or **Deferred** (send to the LLD or epics, with a trigger).

## A. Divergence risks between units

| # | Missing item | Source | Why two units could diverge | Suggest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **LAN frame envelope.** Every frame carries `v`, `kind` and `rid`. Replies correlate on `rid`. The device times out at 5 s and shows "Hub slow". The handshake is `hello{device token, kind: kiosk\|pi, versions, capabilities}`. Unknown fields are ignored. | LLD Hub connection; Pi table; Protocol versioning | Kiosk, Pi, KDS and the owner live view would each invent their own framing and handshake | AD (new, "LAN protocol envelope") |
| 2 | **Lease timings and the per-transaction epoch check.** The lease renews every 3 s and expires after 15 s. Every `apply()` re-reads `lease.epoch` inside the transaction and fails with `NOT_PRIMARY`. A demoted process closes its writer. The standby starts as follower if a fresh lease with a higher epoch exists. | LLD Lease; Role switch | Failover timing (HLD target under 10 s) and fencing semantics would be implemented differently | AD-1 / AD-11 amend |
| 3 | **Promote mode is ambiguous.** HLD says "manual promote in v1". LLD has an `auto_promote` rollout flag. | HLD Risks; LLD Feature flags | Standby builds would differ on whether they self-promote | AD-11 amend (state whether auto-promote exists) |
| 4 | **Batch and manifest format.** Object key `tenants/{t}/branches/{b}/changes/{epoch:08}/{first:012}-{last:012}.zst`. Payload is a msgpack header plus full-row maps keyed by column name. At most 5000 rows per batch. Upload triggers on interval or 500 pending rows. Interval floor is 30 s (cost guard). Presigned PUT valid 10 min. Manifest = `{key, sha256, first, last, epoch}`. A new epoch continues the sequence. | LLD Uploader, Ingester, Migrations; HLD Risks | Uploader, ingester and follower must agree byte for byte | AD-4 amend |
| 5 | **Replayer tolerance rule.** The replayer ignores unknown columns and fills missing ones with defaults. Upsert by `(tbl,row_id)`, and op `D` deletes. The follower writes rows verbatim with the same `seq` and `epoch`. | LLD Migrations; Ingester; Replication | Mixed-version safety depends on this | AD-4 / AD-8 amend |
| 6 | **Command lifecycle.** States are `pending → fetched → applied \| rejected \| expired`. A fetched command goes back to pending after 10 min. Commands carry `schema_version`, and an unparseable command returns `UNSUPPORTED` ("update the branch"). The Hub fetches with `?after=` in id order. Result POST is idempotent. `branch: all` fans out with a `group_id`. | LLD Commands; Poller; Owner app states | Owner PWA, command API and Hub poller each need the same state machine | AD-5 amend |
| 7 | **Dedupe key.** HLD/LLD dedupe on command id (`applied_commands`). AD-14 adds a client idempotency key. The relationship between the two is unstated. | LLD Poller; spine AD-14 | The cloud and the Hub might dedupe on different keys | AD-14 amend (key → command id mapping) |
| 8 | **Entitlement JWT claims and lifecycle.** Claims are `{tenant_id, branch_id, tier, flags, features, upload_interval_s, retention_days, limits, iat, exp}`. Two keys verify and one signs, rotated with a Hub release. A halted subscription gets 7 days of `grace` and then becomes `lapsed`, which means free flags. Rollout flags live in the `features` claim, but the spine says they come "from control plane". | LLD Entitlement; Billing; Feature flags | Hub gating, cloud signing and billing states would each define their own claims | AD-10 / AD-20 amend |
| 9 | **Pairing and revocation.** Pairing returns a 32-byte token once, together with the first JWT. The Pi uses a 6-digit code. Revocation takes effect within one heartbeat, or immediately on the LAN. | LLD Pairing; HLD Security | Revocation SLA and token shape differ per device type | AD-10 amend |
| 10 | **Branch timezone.** Store UTC ms. Apply the branch timezone only for display, opening hours and pricing windows. Windows are expressed as local minutes from midnight, with a midnight wrap. In `days_mask`, bit 0 = Monday. Dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD`. | LLD Conventions; pricing_rules | Hub pricing, owner reports and the pricing editor could disagree | AD-7 amend |
| 11 | **Kiosk clock source.** AD-12 counts down from "last Hub-issued end time" with no rule against using the kiosk wall clock. The LLD says never to use the kiosk's own clock. | LLD Timer "Clock skew"; spine AD-12 | A skewed PC clock would lock too early or too late | AD-12 amend (remaining-ms + monotonic clock) |
| 12 | **Invoice and credit-note numbering.** `invoice_sequences(fy)` is keyed `'FY26-27'`. A number is issued at settle, and a refund issues a credit note. Format, per-branch vs per-GSTIN scope and gaplessness are not specified. | LLD schema; settle/refund intents; Test plan | Must be gap-free and unique for GST, and AD-13 depends on "invoice issued" | AD (new) |
| 13 | **GST and money rounding.** Rates are basis points (`gst_rate_bp`, `percent_bp`). Totals = lines + GST − discount. Rate display rounds to the nearest rupee, but billing stays in paise. Per-line vs per-invoice GST rounding is undefined. Money is "paise (or minor units of branch currency)". | LLD Conventions; schema; Rate resolution | Receipts, mirror reports and the owner PWA could compute different totals | AD-7 amend / new AD |
| 14 | **Error envelope.** `AppError{code, message, field?}`. User text is looked up by code per locale and never sent over the wire. HTTP status only wraps the code. | LLD Conventions; Error codes | UI copy mapping would diverge | AD-7 amend |
| 15 | **Change-log row content.** Each row carries `actor`, `intent_id` and both actors for PIN elevation (`pin_staff_id`). No I/O happens inside the transaction except SQLite. | LLD Intent catalogue; Conventions; HLD Audit | Audit view and replay depend on it | AD-2 amend |
| 16 | **Retention conflict.** The spine (AD-24) keeps money records 8 years. HLD keeps R2 1 yr (Basic) / 3 yr (Premium) and lets mirrors keep what R2 keeps. Hub `changes` are pruned after 90 days once uploaded. | HLD Data rule 6; spine AD-24 | GST retention cannot be met as written | AD-24 amend (resolve conflict) |
| 17 | **Outbound messaging path.** `notify_outbox` rides the change log. The cloud ingester sends via Gupshup (WhatsApp, then SMS) and writes the delivery state back as a command. Consent is required. | LLD Notifications; HLD Integrations | Hub, ingester and command relay share this loop | AD (new) |
| 18 | **Discovery.** mDNS, with a fallback to a stored Hub IP. Devices re-resolve on `epoch.changed`. Jittered backoff of 2–30 s. The mDNS service name is not specified. | LLD Hub connection; HLD Journey 8 | Devices must find the new primary after a promote | AD-11 amend |
| 19 | **Update signing chain.** The Hub caches signed installers and verifies them before serving on the LAN: ed25519 (Tauri updater) plus Authenticode for the Kiosk, ed25519 for the Pi. Install only with no active session. | LLD Kiosk Updates; Pi; HLD Supply chain | Kiosk, Pi and Hub must use the same signature scheme | AD (new) or link Open Q 6 |
| 20 | **Refund approval command.** A refund over the owner threshold returns `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` and needs an owner command to approve. | LLD `refund.create` | A cross-unit flow between Hub and owner PWA | Deferred (epics) |
| 21 | **Rate-limit location.** LLD puts the pairing limits (5/code, 20/IP/h) in an app-level `rate_limits` table. AD-23 puts limits at the Cloudflare edge. | LLD Pairing; spine AD-23 | Two limiters, or none | AD-23 amend |
| 22 | **Android → Windows upgrade.** The Android database exports as a snapshot that the Windows Hub imports on pairing. Android has no tunnel or standby. Limits are enforced from the cached JWT. | LLD Android Hub | Snapshot format must be shared | Deferred (epics; trigger: paid upgrade story) |
