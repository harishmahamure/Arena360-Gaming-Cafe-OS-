Cinema Electric — light theme variants

Owner app + web surfaces (marketing, pricing, onboarding, partner portal). Each light variant sits beside the existing Electric dark. Magenta is split into accent (fill, white ink) and darker accent-text (links, focus ring) so both pass AA on light surfaces. Ratio tables are expandable under each pair. Every computed light pair passes.
