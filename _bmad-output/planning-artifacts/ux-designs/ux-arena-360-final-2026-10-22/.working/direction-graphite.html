Graphite

Calm, flat charcoal — the ggLeap structure, quietly rebranded. Art does the shouting; chrome stays out of the way.

Closest to the reference the user already likes: flat charcoal surfaces, one steel-blue accent, rounded pills, medium-weight Segoe-class type. Everything that isn't game art is grey, so any branch's key art (or branch branding) drops in without fighting the UI. The Hub uses the same neutral base with state colour carried by a thick left stripe plus a word, so colour-blind staff and washed-out monitors still read state.

  • Strengths: Lowest risk; trivial to build in WPF (solid brushes, CornerRadius, no blur); familiar to players coming from ggLeap/Senet cafés; calm for 10-hour counter shifts
  • Weaknesses: Can feel generic / 'another ggLeap clone'; brand identity has to come from logo and art, not the UI; steel-blue on charcoal is lower energy than the market expects in esports-heavy cafés
  • Cheap monitors: Good — mid-grey surfaces avoid crushed blacks on TN panels; 4.5:1+ body text throughout
