Loud esports energy — near-black, acid-lime accent, angled cuts and condensed caps. Feels like a tournament stage.

Speaks directly to the 14–30 competitive crowd: near-black canvas, a single acid-lime accent with hot-magenta reserved for urgency, chamfered (cut-corner) shapes, condensed uppercase Bahnschrift (ships with Windows 10+, so WPF gets it for free). Kiosk is tighter and more 'HUD-like'; the rail becomes stacked telemetry panels. Hub is dense and numeric — monospaced timers and solid state fills so the floor reads like a scoreboard.

  • Strengths: Strongest differentiation vs ggLeap; matches esports branding of Valorant/CS2 cafés; time and urgency read instantly
  • Weaknesses: Lime on black can fatigue counter staff across long shifts; chamfered shapes need Path geometry in WPF (more work than CornerRadius); harder to co-brand for a family-friendly or PS5-heavy branch
  • Cheap monitors: Pure-black areas crush on TN panels and show backlight bleed; Bahnschrift falls back to Arial Narrow on Windows 7 (Kiosk supports Win7 SP1)
