Arena 360 — four visual directions

Same content in each: Kiosk home (hero), HUD 10-min warning state, account menu, and Hub floor view. Pick a direction, or mix parts (e.g. one kiosk look with another Hub look).

1

Graphite

Calm, flat charcoal — the ggLeap structure, quietly rebranded. Art does the shouting; chrome stays out of the way.

Tradeoff: Safest and easiest to build in WPF, but brand personality is thin — the café's identity rests on logo and art.

2

Overdrive

Loud esports energy — near-black, acid-lime accent, angled cuts and condensed caps. Feels like a tournament stage.

Tradeoff: Most exciting and distinctive, but tiring for staff over long shifts and the chamfered shapes plus Win7 font fallback make WPF work harder.

3

Cinema

Premium and cinematic — full-bleed key art, frosted-glass panels, light thin type. The PC feels like a console dashboard.

Tradeoff: Looks the most premium, but glass/blur is costly in WPF on older PCs and contrast becomes art-dependent on cheap monitors.

Open full ↗
4

Chai Break

Warm, bright neighbourhood café — cream paper, saffron and teal, rounded and friendly. A hangout, not a bunker.

Tradeoff: Friendliest and best for staff readability in the Hub, but a bright kiosk glares in dark gaming rooms and may feel less 'gamer' to the core audience.
