Status is colour + wordEvery command-row carries a status-badge: pending (Queued on this device, Waiting for branch) = info, applied = ok, rejected = danger, expired = text-muted. Changes are announced politely (aria-live). Rejections show only “Device/Hub revoked” or “Couldn't apply at branch” + Retry; Expired offers Resend. The app offers only valid targets, so other failures don't reach the list.
Pricing and sendingManage → Pricing has three sections under a tab-control: Plans & rates · Pricing rules · Coupons. Rule rows show schedule, group, branches and status. Price, product and plan changes send with no MFA prompt; fresh MFA is kept for staff users and roles, revoking a device or Hub, and backup restore.
Themes and rhythmLight theme maps every token to its -light twin: accent fills carry white ink, focus is a single 2px accent-text ring. Dark keeps the double ring (accent inner, white outer). Gutter 16 · header 64 · tab bar 64 with Manage active · card padding 24 · 40 between title and content. Dashed lines in frames 1 and 5 mark the 844 fold.
