Pricing

Each branch is billed separately, per month. Every plan runs your café offline.

CafésPartners

Free

₹0 per branch per month

One branch, supported by advertising. No card required.

  • Hub on an Android tablet or phone
  • Up to 20 devices and 3 users
  • POS, inventory, shifts and GST invoices
  • Advertising on the Hub banner
Choose Free

Basic

₹200 per branch per month

Removes advertising and adds the cloud.

  • Hub on Windows PC or Android
  • Up to 60 devices, unlimited users
  • Kiosk food orders and kitchen display
  • Owner app, cloud backup, multi-branch
Choose Basic

Premium

₹1,999 per branch per month

Adds resilience and remote access.

  • Up to 200 devices
  • Live owner view of the floor
  • Off-site standby and remote devices
  • 1-minute cloud backup, 3-year retention
Choose Premium

Frequently asked questions

What happens when the internet is down?

Every Hub and kiosk feature continues to work on the café network. Cloud features queue and resume when the connection returns.

What happens if I cancel?

[open: cancellation and refund terms]

Who owns my data?

You may export the full branch database and all reports at any time, including on the Free plan.

Can I change plans later?

Upgrades and downgrades take effect mid-cycle and are prorated. Upgrading from Free removes advertising within one sync with the cloud.
