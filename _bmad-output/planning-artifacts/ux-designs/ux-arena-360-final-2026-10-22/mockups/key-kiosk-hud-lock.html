  • Warning toasts at 10, 5 and 1 min (plus low balance) come with a sound and stay until dismissed. They never steal focus from the game.
  • The kiosk time chip turns amber with a ⚠ at 10 min in the shell too. The HUD pill hides in fullscreen-exclusive games and no overlay is forced over the game; at 5 and 1 min the kiosk instead flashes its taskbar button and plays the warning sound.
  • Known limitation (accepted): in true fullscreen-exclusive games the taskbar flash may be invisible, so the 5- and 1-min warnings can be effectively sound-only for deaf and hard-of-hearing players. The 10-min chip warning, the grace lock screen and Extend by hotkey still apply (EXPERIENCE.md Accessibility Floor).
  • The hotkey opens the expanded HUD (extend-options) with focus on the first option.

  • Grace defaults to 2 min (branch setting). The countdown uses tabular digits and is announced at milestones, not on every tick.
  • Extend stays available during grace (button or Ctrl+Shift+E). A successful Extend dismisses the lock; while the Hub is unreachable it is disabled with "Counter is reconnecting — try again shortly."
  • After grace, the per-branch time-up mode applies: (a) kill allow-listed processes, sign out accounts, return to attract; or (b) keep the lock over the paused game for staff.
