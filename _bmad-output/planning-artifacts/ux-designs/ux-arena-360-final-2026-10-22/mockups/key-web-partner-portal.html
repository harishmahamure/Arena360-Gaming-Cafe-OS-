Payout terms10% of subscription revenue for each month a referred café stays active and paying. Paid by bank transfer by the 10th for the previous month. A balance below ₹500 shows “Pending — below ₹500 minimum, rolls over” and is added to next month. TDS is deducted and shown per payout.
GST invoice and KYCGST-registered partners upload a GST invoice for each payout; until then the row shows “Upload GST invoice” and the payout is held. After approval, KYC (PAN, bank details, GSTIN optional) is asked for before the first payout: “KYC needed before first payout”.
Light themeHub tones map to the light set (hub-bg and hub-header → bg-light, hub-tile → surface-light, hub-line → line-light). Links and the focus ring use accent-text-light; warning ink and edges use warning-text-light. Focus is shown on “Copy link” (A) and “Submit KYC” (B).
