Frequently asked questions

What is your refund policy?

You receive a full refund if you cancel within 7 days of your first payment.

After that, you may cancel at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, monthly or yearly, and the branch then moves to Free. Partial refunds are not issued.

Is GST included in the price?

No. Prices are shown exclusive of GST. 18% GST is added and itemised on each invoice.

What happens when the internet is down?

Every Hub and Kiosk feature keeps working on the café network. Cloud features queue and resume when the connection returns.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, in the Owner app under Subscription. Yearly billing is 10× the monthly price, so two months are free.

Who owns my data?

You do. Export the full branch database and all reports at any time, including on Free.

Are there ads on my players’ screens?

No. On Free, ads appear only on the Android Hub at the counter. Paid plans have no ads.

Pricing

For resellers and installers who set up Arena 360 for cafés.

CafésPartners

Partner programme

Commission
10% of the subscription revenue of each café you refer, for every month that café stays active and paying.
How referrals count
A café counts as yours when it signs up with your referral link or code.
Territory
Leads are not exclusive by territory.

Payout terms

Schedule
Monthly bank transfer, made by the 10th for the previous month.
Minimum
₹500. A lower balance rolls over to the next month.
Tax
TDS is deducted from each payout. GST-registered partners upload a GST invoice before payout.
KYC
After approval, add your PAN and bank details (GSTIN optional) before your first payout.
Apply to become a partner

You’ll need your name, phone (verified by OTP), email, city, business name, partner type (reseller or installer) and the number of cafés you serve. We notify approval on WhatsApp with your portal login link.

Worked example

Commission earned in October, paid by 10 November.

Café A · 2 branches on Basic₹400.00
Café B · 3 branches on Premium₹5,997.00
Subscription revenue (excl. GST)₹6,397.00
Commission at 10%₹639.70
TDS deductedAs applicable
Paid by 10 November₹639.70 less TDS

If Café B had not signed up, October’s commission would be ₹40.00. That is below the ₹500 minimum, so it would roll over to November.
