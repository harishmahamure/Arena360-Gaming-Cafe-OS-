---
title: My branch shows offline in the Owner app
summary: Why the Owner app can show a branch as offline, and how to bring it back.
category: troubleshooting
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

The Owner app shows what your Hub sends to the cloud. A branch shows offline when the cloud has not heard from its Hub for a while. The café itself keeps running without internet.

1. Check the Hub is switched on and the café's internet is working.
2. Look at the Hub's header. It shows whether the link to the cloud is down.
3. Restart the router, then the Hub.
4. Once the link returns the Hub sends what it missed, so the Owner app catches up without any action.

The Owner app needs a paid plan. See [Pricing](/pricing).
