---
title: The Hub is slow
summary: What to check when the floor, POS or reports feel slow.
category: troubleshooting
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

1. Close other programs on the counter PC. The Hub should have the PC mostly to itself.
2. Restart the Hub. Kiosks carry on their sessions while it restarts.
3. Check free disk space. Keep a few gigabytes free for the database and backups.
4. Use a wired network connection for the counter PC if you can.
5. Check that no old devices you no longer use are still paired, and that you are within your plan's device limit.

If it stays slow, send the details through the support channel for your plan below.
