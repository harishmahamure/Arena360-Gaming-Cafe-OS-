---
title: Install the Hub on Android
summary: Run a free single-branch café from an Android phone or tablet.
category: setup
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

The Android Hub is for a free, single-branch café. It needs Android 9 or later, 3 GB of RAM and 20 GB of free storage.

1. Open **Android Hub** on the [Downloads](/downloads) page and choose **Get it on Google Play**. Google Play installs and updates the app, so there is no file to check.
2. Open the app and type the 8-character pairing code shown on the setup page.
3. Allow the Hub to run in the background when Android asks. Android may otherwise pause it to save battery, and kiosks lose their Hub.
4. Keep the device on the same Wi-Fi as your gaming PCs, and plugged in.
