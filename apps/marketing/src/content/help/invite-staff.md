---
title: Invite staff
summary: Add a manager or counter staff by phone number and give them a role.
category: setup
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

Staff sign in to the Hub with their own account, so the audit log shows who did what.

1. Open **Staff** on the Hub, or invite from the setup page.
2. Enter the person's phone number and choose a role. A role decides what they can do; manager actions need a manager PIN.
3. They receive a one-time setup link by message and set their own password and PIN.

The Free plan allows 3 users. Basic and Premium have no user limit.
