---
title: The kiosk cannot find the Hub
summary: What to check when a gaming PC does not see your Hub on the network.
category: troubleshooting
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

1. Check the Hub is running on the counter PC or Android device.
2. Check the gaming PC and the Hub are on the same network. A guest Wi-Fi or a second router often separates them.
3. Type the Hub's IP address on the Kiosk instead of waiting for it to find the Hub. The Hub shows its address under **System**.
4. Allow the Hub through the Windows firewall on the counter PC if Windows asked and the prompt was dismissed.
5. Restart the Kiosk. A kiosk that was paired before keeps its pairing.
