---
title: Pair a kiosk with your Hub
summary: Install the Kiosk on a gaming PC and connect it to the Hub with a 6-digit code.
category: setup
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

The Kiosk is the program on each gaming PC. It locks the PC and shows the player screen.

1. Download **Windows Kiosk** from [Downloads](/downloads), check its SHA-256, and run the installer on the gaming PC. It needs Windows 10 version 1803 to Windows 11, 64-bit, with 4 GB of RAM.
2. The Kiosk looks for the Hub on your network. If it finds your Hub, choose it. If not, type the Hub's IP address.
3. Pair with the 6-digit code shown on the Hub, or with a manager PIN.
4. The PC appears on the Floor view. Put it in a device group so it gets the right rate.

If the Kiosk does not find the Hub, see [the kiosk cannot find the Hub](/help/kiosk-cannot-find-the-hub).
