---
title: The printer is offline
summary: Fixes for a receipt or kitchen printer that does not print.
category: troubleshooting
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

1. Check the printer has power, paper and a closed lid, and that its cable or network connection is in place.
2. For a network printer, check its IP address has not changed. Give the printer a fixed address in your router.
3. On the Hub, run a test print from **Settings**. If it prints, the problem was the connection.
4. Check the paper width on the Hub matches the roll.
5. Unplug the printer for ten seconds, plug it in again and print again.

Printers we have checked are on the [hardware compatibility list](/help#hardware-compatibility). To set one up again from the start, see [set up a printer and a cash drawer](/help/set-up-a-printer-and-cash-drawer).
