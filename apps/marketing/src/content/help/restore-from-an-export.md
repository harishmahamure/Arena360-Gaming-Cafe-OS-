---
title: Restore from an export
summary: Bring a branch back on a new Hub from an export file.
category: troubleshooting
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

An export is a file the Hub saves of your whole branch. Keep daily exports on a drive that is not the counter PC.

1. Install the Hub on the new PC and pair it. See [install the Hub on Windows](/help/install-the-hub-on-windows).
2. Open **Restore from file** and choose the export.
3. The Hub shows what is in the file, such as the branch and its date. Check it is the one you want.
4. Confirm. The restored branch starts a new epoch, so devices of the old Hub must pair again.

If the export is from a Hub that is still running, switch that Hub off first.
