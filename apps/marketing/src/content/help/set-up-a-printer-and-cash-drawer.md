---
title: Set up a printer and a cash drawer
summary: Connect an ESC/POS receipt printer, run a test print, and have the drawer open on cash sales.
category: setup
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---

Any ESC/POS receipt printer works. The [hardware compatibility list](/help#hardware-compatibility) shows the models we have checked.

1. Connect the printer by USB, over your network (LAN) or, on Android, by Bluetooth, and load 58 mm or 80 mm paper.
2. On the Hub, open **Settings**. The Hub lists the printers it finds; choose yours.
3. Choose the paper width that matches the roll, then run a test print.
4. Plug the cash drawer into the printer's drawer port. The drawer opens on cash sales.

If the test print does not come out, see [the printer is offline](/help/printer-offline).
