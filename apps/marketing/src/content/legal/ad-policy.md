Arena 360 is free for one branch, and the Free plan on the Android Hub is paid for by ads. This policy says where they appear and what they never are.

## Where ads appear

Ads appear only on the Free Android Hub, at the counter. They never appear on player screens: not on a Kiosk, not on a Console TV, and not in the Owner app.

## Which ads are never shown

Ads for adult content, gambling and alcohol are excluded. Each branch also has a content rating that limits what the ad network may show.

## What ads can and cannot do

An ad has its own banner slot that nothing can cover or move. A full-screen ad is shown only when staff come back to the Hub from being away, never in the middle of a sale. Rewarded ads, which pay people to watch, are not used.

## What we record

We record that an ad was requested and shown, without any advertising ID, and we send those counts to the cloud.

## Removing ads

Ads are removed within one heartbeat of upgrading to a paid plan. If a paid plan lapses, the branch drops to Free and ads return.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish a new version here with its effective date, and list the change below.
