These terms apply when Arena 360 handles personal data for a café. The café decides why and how its players' data is used (the controller). Arena 360 handles that data on the café's behalf (the processor), only to run the service.

## What we handle

Players' names and phone numbers, and the sessions, orders and balances tied to them, held on the café's Hub. The cloud holds only what the Hub sends it: summaries, backups the owner has enabled, and message deliveries.

## What we do with it

We use it only to run the café's service: to show it to the café's staff, to send the receipts and notices the café has chosen, and to keep backups. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

## Where it is kept

Cloud data is hosted in India. Backups are kept for the plan's retention period.

## Our staff

Arena 360 staff look at a café's data only to give support the café has asked for.

## Helpers we use

We use a small number of providers to host the cloud, to send messages and to take payments. They handle data only to provide those services to us.

## Your control

The café can export the full branch database and every report at any time, on every plan. A player can be erased from the Hub, and their personal data cannot be recovered afterwards.

## If something goes wrong

If we learn that personal data we hold for a café has been exposed, we tell the owner without undue delay, with what happened and what we are doing about it.

## Changes to these terms

When we change these terms we publish a new version here with its effective date, and list the change below.
