This policy says what Arena 360 keeps, where, and why. The same version number is recorded with a player's consent when they sign up at a Kiosk.

## What the café's Hub keeps

Each branch's data lives on the café's own Hub: players' names and phone numbers, sessions, sales, wallets and reports. Personal details are stored encrypted and shown only to staff who need them.

## What the cloud keeps

The cloud keeps the owner's account (phone number, sign-in details and branch names), the branch's licence and billing records, and the summaries and backups the Hub sends. Cloud data is hosted in India.

## Players

When a player signs up at a Kiosk we keep their name and phone number so the branch can find their account and message them about it. We send service messages on WhatsApp or SMS, such as receipts and balance notices, and we do not send marketing from the sign-up form.

## Seeing and erasing your details

A player can ask at the counter to see or delete their details. When a branch erases a player, their personal data is erased by destroying the key that protects it, and it cannot be recovered. Records the café must keep for tax, such as invoices, stay.

## This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits from the site to sign-up and on to a live branch without recording who the visitor is. Help pages never ask for personal details.

## Ads

Ads appear only on the Free Android Hub at the counter. See the [Ad policy](/legal/ad-policy).

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish a new version here with its effective date, and list the change below.
