## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time. Your plan stays active to the end of the paid period, then the branch drops to Free. You can have a full refund within 7 days of your first payment. After that there are no partial refunds.

## When the internet is down

The Hub runs your floor without the internet. Features that need the cloud, such as the Owner app and cloud backup, catch up when the connection is back.
